# Insights
GDP and Recession: GDP stays mostly lower and more changeable when there is a recession showing economic worry. Times without a recession have more steady GDP values.
Seasonality:Seasonality does not seem to have an impact on total car sales.. There are clear jumps in sales during April and December.
Consumer Confidence and Vehicle Prices: In times of recession higher consumer confidence is linked to car sales while higher car prices usually lead to less demand. This shows how important it is for people to feel good about the economy and how much they can afford.
Advertising Expenditure:XYZAutomotives spends 82.7% of its advertising money when there is no recession and only 17.3% when there is a recession. This shows that the company cuts back on costs during times and spends more when the economy is better. Advertising Focus:During recessions the advertising is mostly focused on cars, which is a smart way to reach people who are looking for deals.
Unemployment and Automobile Sales:Car sales go down when more people are out of work especially when unemployment goes above 3 percent. SuperMiniCars handle this better, than others while SmallFamilyCars stay steady at first. MediumFamilyCars face a steady drop as unemployment goes up.
